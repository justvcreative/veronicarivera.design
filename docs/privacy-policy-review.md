# Privacy Policy: review and final draft

November 8, 2026 · For Veronica to review before any Penpot or site changes. Not legal advice.

## Bottom line

- **No cookie banner needed.** The site sets no cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookie-free. The only thing stored on a visitor's device is the Light/Dark choice they make themselves, which counts as "strictly necessary" for a feature they asked for, so it needs no consent.
- **Keep the plain-language draft** (below). It's built on my Penpot draft, with the few useful pieces from the two templates added.
- **Decided:** keep messages up to 12 months; handle deletion requests within 30 days. Revisit after you've used Cloudflare's dashboard for a while.

## The Upcounsel template

Written for a business with user accounts, cookies, marketing email and investors. Most of it doesn't apply to you, and some of it would make the policy *wrong* for your site.

| Keep the idea | Leave out |
| --- | --- |
| Children under 13 (a short section is standard and expected) | "By using the site you consent…" (you don't need it, and it reads as a trap) |
| A one-line security note ("no method is 100% secure") | Cookies, accounts, passwords, marketing email, opt-outs: you have none |
| Sharing only when the law requires it | Selling data in a merger, sharing "non-personal" data with advertisers |
| Changes section with a date | "We'll email you about changes": you have no accounts to email |

## The Google AI draft

Close to mine, and the plain tone is right. Fix these before using any of it:

- **Wrong:** "does not use… local storage." The site does store the theme choice in local storage. Say so plainly (it's harmless and stays on the device).
- **Wrong:** "web traffic routes through Cloudflare's network." It doesn't: GitHub serves the site, and only the small analytics script loads from Cloudflare. Squarespace only answers the domain lookup.
- **Missing:** the form also sends the topic you pick ("What's it about?"). Formspree's spam filter is worth a mention.
- **Missing:** Cloudflare as a named service with its own privacy policy; children under 13; LinkedIn, the résumé on Google Docs, the YouTube news video, Unsplash, and the live client sites in the links list.
- **Good, keep:** naming Gmail for direct email, and a plain sentence about privacy rights under laws like the GDPR (EU) and CCPA (California).
- **Tone:** it switches between "we" and "I". Use "I" throughout; it's your personal site.

## Reading level

Aim for grade 5–8: short sentences, everyday words, "I" and "you", one idea per paragraph, headings phrased as the question a visitor has. Name companies and link their policies rather than describing their legal terms.

## Final draft: Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy**
Effective November 8, 2026

This is my personal portfolio. It collects as little as it can, and I never sell or share what it does collect.

### Who runs this site
I'm Veronica Rivera, a product designer and design technologist in Louisville, Kentucky. Questions about this policy go to justvcreative@gmail.com.

### What I collect when you contact me
If you send a message through the contact form, it collects:
- your name
- your email address
- what your message is about
- your message

I use these only to read and reply to your message.

The form is run by **Formspree**. Formspree receives each message, checks it for spam, emails it to me and keeps a copy in my Formspree account. See Formspree's privacy policy. If you email me directly instead, your message goes to my Gmail account, run by Google.

### How long I keep messages
I keep messages for up to 12 months. Then I delete them from my email and from Formspree.

### Analytics
I use **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It does not use cookies, and it does not follow you to other websites. It records:
- which pages were viewed and how fast they loaded
- the website that sent you here, if any
- your browser, operating system and type of device
- your country

I can't use it to tell who you are. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

### Cookies and your theme choice
This site doesn't use cookies. If you pick Light or Dark in the theme menu, your browser saves that choice so the next page matches. It stays on your device, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

### Hosting
The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep a short record of visitors' IP addresses to keep the site secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

### Links to other websites
Some links take you to other websites, like my résumé on Google Docs, my LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram and TikTok profiles, a news video on YouTube, and the live websites in my case studies. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy doesn't cover them.

### Children
This site isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has sent me a message, email me and I'll delete it.

### Keeping your information safe
I use trusted services and keep my accounts secure. No website or email service is 100% safe, though, so please don't send passwords or other sensitive details through the form.

### When I might share information
I don't sell, rent or trade your information. I would only share it if the law requires me to.

### Your choices
You can ask me what information I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Depending on where you live, laws like the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California may give you these rights; I'll honor them for everyone. Email justvcreative@gmail.com, and I'll take care of it within 30 days.

### Changes to this policy
If I change how this site handles your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
